Box condition

What shape is your box in?

These tiers mirror our buyers' Mint / Dinged / Damaged guidelines. Sealed and crisp earns the most; light wear earns a little less; broken seals, stains, writing or recalled lots can't be accepted. Leave tape and stickers on — we remove them safely.

Mint

Store-shelf quality — no scuffs, scratches or adhesive residue, and no dents, dings or crushed corners.

Full payout

Dinged

Mostly mint: rips or tears under ½″ and minor corner dings, with the name, expiration and count still readable.

~15% less

Acceptable damage

Surface cuts that don’t go through the cardboard, larger dents, or peeling outer layer that doesn’t hide key info. Small ink marks okay — don’t clean them.

Up to ~45% less

Can't accept

Any seal damage, smashed or illegible boxes, moisture, stains, mold, odors, punctures, or any writing. Recalled lots and insurance-sourced too.

Not eligible
Full condition guidelines

Mint. Store-shelf quality — exactly as it would look on a pharmacy shelf. Entirely free of cosmetic damage (scuffs, scratches, adhesive residue) and structural wear (dents, dings, crushed corners).

Dinged. Mostly mint with minor handling or transit wear. Small rips or tears under ½ inch and minor corner dings are fine at a reduced rate, as long as the damage is localized and doesn't obscure the product name, expiration date or box count.

Acceptable damage. Larger defects that don't compromise the contents — surface cuts that don't penetrate the cardboard, larger corner dents, or peeling of the outer layer that doesn't hide key info. Small incidental ink or marker marks are okay; do not try to clean them, as smudging can cause a rejection.

Can't accept. Any sign of seal damage, shifting, stretching or tearing; severe structural damage or smashed boxes; illegible information; moisture, water stains, warping, mold or pest activity; strong smoke or chemical odors; food or organic stains (even small ones); punctures large enough to insert or remove contents; or any intentional writing such as names, initials, symbols or dates. Recalled lots and insurance-sourced product are also declined.
